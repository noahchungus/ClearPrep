How scoring works

ClearPrep has two layers. The numbers come from a rule-based scorer with no AI in it: named checks over word lists and patterns that you can read below (the exact lists it runs). The written coaching comes from an AI coach, which reads your answer and those findings and talks you through them.

Where the AI coach fits

  • It writes your feedback, follow-up questions, and debrief. It never changes your scores.
  • It can be wrong, so check its advice against your highlighted answer.
  • When it’s on, your answers and any pasted job text go to a third-party AI service. This app stores nothing. Turn it off on the practice page to stay fully local.

The formula

Each answer is scored on five dimensions from 0 to 100. A dimension is a list of checks. Each check has a maximum number of points, earns some of them, and records why and which words of yours triggered it:

dimension score = 100 × (points earned ÷ points possible)

Some checks can only subtract (vague wording, generic “why us” phrases, asking about pay too early). The overall score is a weighted average:

What limits it

  • Scores measure structure and delivery, not whether your story is impressive or a technical answer is correct.
  • The checks look for patterns, so unusual phrasing can be missed. See the measured accuracy below.
  • Company details come only from text you paste. Nothing is looked up.

STAR detection

STAR = Situation, Task, Action, Result. The detector looks for cue phrases (regular expressions) and checks where in the answer they appear: situation cues count fully in the first {{ (s.cues.situation.zone[1] * 100)|int }}% of the answer, result cues in the last {{ ((1 - s.cues.result.zone[0]) * 100)|int }}%. A cue outside its expected zone counts half. A part is “present” when the weighted cue count reaches {{ s.present_threshold|int }}; a lower non-zero weight is “weak”. Actions also get credit for first-person action verbs (“I built…”); results get credit for a number in the closing half.
