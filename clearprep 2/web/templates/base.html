ClearPrep — built by a community college student, for other students getting ready to interview.
Developed by Noah Chung

AI-powered coaching, transparent scores. Every answer gets written feedback from an AI coach, which can be wrong, so treat it as advice. The scores come from named checks you can inspect on How scoring works.

Privacy: no accounts, no database, and this app stores nothing. Your history lives only in this browser. With the AI coach on, your answers and any pasted job or company text are sent to a third-party AI service to write the feedback; turn it off on the practice page to keep everything local.
