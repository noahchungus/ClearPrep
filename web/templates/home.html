Straight talk

AI-powered feedback on every answer

An AI coach reads each answer and writes personal feedback: what worked, what to change, and a better way to say it. It never invents facts about you or your employer.

Scores you can inspect

The numbers come from named, rule-based checks you can read on the How scoring works page. The AI writes feedback but never changes your scores. It can still be wrong, so treat it as advice.

You stay in control

No accounts and nothing stored on the server; your history lives in your browser. With the AI coach on, your answers are sent to a third-party AI service to write feedback. Turn it off for a fully local, rule-based session.

Ready when you are.
